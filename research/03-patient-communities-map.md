# 03 — Existing MPN & Myelofibrosis Patient Communities Map

> **Dossier Purpose (`rare-disease-atlas.github.io`):** Strategic directory and ethical engagement playbook for founders, public health researchers, and AI agents.
>
> **Core Principle — Do Not Reinvent the Gathering Place:** Myelofibrosis is a rare disease (~15,000–25,000 US patients), and patients are *already* congregated in high-trust, deeply moderated digital communities and registries. Building yet another standalone "patient social network" fragments support and wastes founder capital. Instead, **listen respectfully, validate real pain points, and partner formally with existing foundations and moderators**.

## 1. Verified Directory of Active MPN & Myelofibrosis Communities (2026 Status)

### A. Peer-to-Peer Forums & High-Signal Discussion Communities

#### 1. HealthUnlocked — MPN Voice Community
- **URL:** `https://healthunlocked.com/mpnvoice` (Charity portal: `https://www.mpnvoice.org.uk`)
- **Status & Approximate Scale:** **Active** — The largest dedicated English-language MPN peer forum globally (**~15,000+ registered community members** across ET, PV, and MF, with dozens of daily posts and comments from UK, US, EU, and Commonwealth patients).
- **Primary Topics & Pain Points Discussed:**
  - Managing crushing MF-related **fatigue**, night sweats, bone pain, pruritus (aquagenic itching), and splenic discomfort/early satiety.
  - Real-world tolerability trade-offs when switching from **ruxolitinib (*Jakafi*)** to **momelotinib (*Ojjaara*)** for anemia/transfusion burden, or **pegylated interferon (*Besremi* / *Pegasys*)** debates in earlier-stage `JAK2 V617F` disease.
  - Anxiety around **ET/PV $\rightarrow$ Myelofibrosis progression** ("bone marrow biopsy reticulin grade anxiety") and **allo-HSCT timing decisions** in patients aged 60–72.
  - Frustration with generalist hematologists dismissing debilitating constitutional symptoms when blood counts look "acceptable."

#### 2. Reddit — `r/mpn`
- **URL:** `https://www.reddit.com/r/mpn/`
- **Status & Approximate Scale:** **Active** — Prominent, well-moderated subreddit (**several thousand subscribers**, active daily/weekly threads, curated wiki/FAQ pointing to NCCN guidelines and MPN specialists).
- **Primary Topics & Pain Points Discussed:**
  - Newly diagnosed patients (including younger/middle-aged `JAK2 V617F` patients) trying to interpret NGS panels (`VAF %`, `ASXL1`, `TET2`), bone marrow biopsy pathology reports, and DIPSS/MIPSS70 risk scores.
  - Navigating **US specialty pharmacy prior authorizations, copay accumulators, and Medicare Part D out-of-pocket costs** for $15,000–$25,000+/month oral JAK inhibitors.
  - Seeking referrals to true **MPN Specialists** vs. general community oncologists.
  - Caregiver threads supporting older parents facing transfusion dependency or stem cell transplant recovery.

#### 3. Smart Patients — MPN Community
- **URL:** `https://www.smartpatients.com` (search/join Myeloproliferative Neoplasms / Blood Cancer communities)
- **Status & Approximate Scale:** **Active (Registration-Gated Peer Forum)** — Structured, high-literacy patient and caregiver discussion threads with integrated clinical trial search tools.
- **Primary Topics & Pain Points Discussed:**
  - Deep-dive patient comparisons of **Phase 1–3 clinical trial protocols** (inclusion criteria, platelet cutoffs, washout anxieties when tapering ruxolitinib to enter a trial, travel burden to academic trial sites).
  - Detailed longitudinal tracking of hemoglobin, platelet counts, spleen palpation measurements, and side-effect mitigation strategies.

#### 4. MPN Cancer Connection & Private Facebook Support Groups
- **URL:** `https://mpncancerconnection.org` (plus large private Facebook groups such as *Myelofibrosis Private Support Group*, *MPN Support Group*, and *Bone Marrow & Stem Cell Transplant Support*)
- **Status & Approximate Scale:** **Active** — Combined private Facebook ecosystems encompass **thousands of MF-specific patients and caregivers** globally, with high daily emotional and practical support activity.
- **Primary Topics & Pain Points Discussed:**
  - Day-to-day caregiving realities, blood transfusion clinic logistics, infection precautions, splenic pain/diet adaptations, GVHD management post-transplant, and grief/palliative transitions after JAK inhibitor failure.

### B. Research Foundations, Registries & Structured Patient Hubs

#### 5. MPN Research Foundation (MPNRF) & *MPN PROGRESSion Registry®* *(plus `myMPN` Legacy Note)*
- **URL:** `https://www.mpnresearchfoundation.org` | Registry ClinicalTrials.gov ID: **`NCT07362225`**
- **Status & Registry Evolution (Verified 2026):**
  - **Active Flagship Foundation.**
  - **Crucial Registry Update (`myMPN` $\rightarrow$ `MPN PROGRESSion Registry®`):** MPNRF originally pioneered the **`myMPN` Patient Registry** in September 2017 on genetic/survey infrastructure to collect self-reported outcomes. Building on those lessons, as of **late 2025/2026**, MPNRF's primary active data engine is the modernized **`MPN PROGRESSion Registry®` (`NCT07362225`)**—a direct-to-patient prospective/retrospective study targeting **~5,000 MPN participants** over 5–10 years by linking **automated Electronic Health Records (EHRs), longitudinal PROs, and insurance claims**.
- **How Builders Can Partner:** MPNRF runs structured **Patient Advisory Panels**, co-designs patient-centric clinical trial protocols with biopharma sponsors, partnered with Blood Cancer United (July 2025) on clinical trial navigation, and licenses/collaborates on de-identified registry insights for progression biomarker research.

#### 6. Blood Cancer United *(formerly The Leukemia & Lymphoma Society / LLS — Rebranded August 2025)*
- **URL:** `https://www.bloodcancerunited.org`
- **Status & Approximate Scale:** **Active (Major National Hub)** — Rebranded from LLS to **Blood Cancer United** in August 2025 explicitly so patients with MPNs, MDS, and myeloma know they are fully represented alongside leukemia/lymphoma. Operates **Blood Cancer United Community** (gated peer network), one-on-one **Information Specialists**, **Clinical Trial Support Center (CTSC)** nurse navigators, and financial copay assistance.
- **Primary Pain Points Addressed:** Clinical trial matching bottlenecks (bridging community patients into academic `JAK2 V617F` / combination trials) and catastrophic drug/transplant financial toxicity.

#### 7. HealthTree Foundation — HealthTree for Myelofibrosis (Cure Hub)
- **URL:** `https://healthtree.org/myelofibrosis`
- **Status & Approximate Scale:** **Active & Rapidly Growing** — Combines patient education ("HealthTree University"), peer coaching, clinical trial finder, and **HealthTree Cure Hub** (a patient-controlled data portal aggregating EHR labs, genetics, and real-world treatment outcomes across hematologic malignancies).
- **Why Study This Model:** HealthTree is a textbook case study for Track A founders showing how giving patients real utility (visualizing their own longitudinal labs + matching to trials/twins) earns high-retention, research-grade longitudinal data consent.

#### 8. MPN Advocacy & Education International
- **URL:** `https://mpnadvocacy.com`
- **Status & Approximate Scale:** **Active** — Hosts frequent regional US in-person & virtual **Patient & Caregiver Symposia** (including dedicated Women & MPN and diversity/access programs) featuring top MPN KOLs alongside pharmaceutical/biotech educational exhibitors.
- **Primary Focus:** Health equity, closing the diagnostic gap between community hematology and academic MPN centers, and direct Q&A between patients and trial investigators.

#### 9. Patient Power (MPN Hub) & PV Reporter
- **URLs:** `https://www.patientpower.info/myeloproliferative-neoplasms` | `https://pvreporter.com`
- **Status & Approximate Scale:** **Active Educational & Patient-Journalism Hubs** — Patient Power produces video interviews/town halls pairing MPN patients with leading investigators after every ASH/ASCO/EHA congress; **PV Reporter** (founded by MPN patient advocate David Wallace) serves as a widely bookmarked "one-stop" resource directory for MPN clinical trials, Interferon/JAKi news, and specialist directories.

## 2. Recurring Unmet Patient Pain Points Synthesized Across Communities

Founders and AI agents scanning patient narratives consistently miss four ground-truth realities that rarely show up in clean clinical review papers:

1. **The "Washout Terror" Blocking Clinical Trial Enrollment:**
   - Many Phase 1/2 relapsed/refractory trials historically required a **14-to-28-day ruxolitinib washout** before Day 1 of experimental therapy. Patients know stopping ruxolitinib causes agonizing spleen rebound, drenching fevers, and rapid decompensation—so they refuse monotherapy salvage trials unless **add-on designs** or **rapid-overlap bridging protocols** are used.
2. **Anemia & Transfusion Time-Toxicity:**
   - Becoming RBC-transfusion-dependent doesn't just worsen prognosis—it chains a 68-year-old patient and their spouse to weekly/biweekly 6-hour cross-match and infusion-chair visits, iron overload anxiety, and crushing pre-transfusion exhaustion.
3. **Symptom vs. Spleen Disconnect:**
   - Patients care deeply about **fatigue, brain fog, bone pain, itching, and transfusion freedom**—not merely whether an MRI shows a 35% vs. 30% spleen volume change. Tools and drugs that improve lived daily function win fiercely loyal patient advocacy.
4. **Specialist Desert:**
   - Outside ~30–40 academic MPN centers in the US/EU, general hematologist-oncologists treat hundreds of breast/lung/colon cancer cases for every 1 myelofibrosis patient—leading to delayed MIPSS70+ NGS testing, premature or overly late JAKi switching, and missed transplant windows.

## 3. Ethical Engagement Playbook for Founders, Researchers & AI Agents

> [!CAUTION]
> **Hard Boundaries in Rare-Disease Patient Communities:**
> - **NEVER** deploy automated bots to post, DM, or cold-solicit patients inside `r/mpn`, HealthUnlocked, Smart Patients, or private Facebook groups. Moderators enforce strict anti-spam/anti-self-promotion rules and ban violators immediately.
> - **NEVER** scrape identifiable usernames, health histories, or private/registration-gated forum posts to train commercial models or build marketing lists without explicit IRB oversight and platform/user consent.

### How to Listen, Validate & Partner Respectfully

1. **Passive Aggregate Problem Discovery (Public Threads Only):**
   - Read public threads on `r/mpn` and `healthunlocked.com/mpnvoice` strictly to calibrate *clinical empathy and problem selection* (e.g., understanding why daily diary fatigue or washout periods fail patients)—never storing PII or quoting vulnerable individuals without consent.
2. **Go Through the Front Door — Moderator & Foundation Partnerships:**
   - Reach out transparently to **MPN Research Foundation**, **MPN Advocacy & Education International**, **MPN Voice**, **HealthTree**, or `r/mpn` mod-mail *before* posting any survey or interview request:
     - State your institutional/company identity, funding source, IRB determination (if human-subjects research), and exactly how findings will be shared back with the community in plain language.
3. **Compensate Patient Advisors Fairly (Fair-Market-Value Patient Engagement):**
   - When convening a **Patient Advisory Board (PAB)** to co-design an ePRO app, external control registry, or Phase 1b trial protocol, compensate patient and caregiver advisors at standard rare-disease fair market value (**typically $100–$200/hour**), schedule short low-fatigue video sessions (30–45 minutes), and close the loop by sharing how their feedback changed your product or trial design.
