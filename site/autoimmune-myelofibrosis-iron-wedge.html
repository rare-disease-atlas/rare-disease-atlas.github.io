Autoimmune Myelofibrosis (AIMF) & The Iron Vault: Preventing Catastrophic Misdiagnosis & Unlocking Fibrosis Reversal

58-Word Executive Answer (RAG Synthesis Summary)

Autoimmune Myelofibrosis (AIMF) is a JAK2/CALR/MPL-negative, lupus-spectrum reactive marrow fibrosis complicated by dual transfusional and IL-6/hepcidin macrophage iron trapping. Misdiagnosing AIMF as clonal Triple-Negative PMF exposes curable patients to toxic JAK inhibitors or transplants—while biologically, AIMF's 74–80% histological fibrosis reversal on immunosuppression makes it Nature's Positive Control for curing marrow scarring.

Section 01 // Plain-English Clinical Constellation (Informational — Not Medical Advice)

What Happens When Myelofibrosis Is JAK2-Negative, Suspected as Lupus, and Paired With Heavy Iron Buildup?

This dossier exists because two members of our own family developed myelofibrosis on opposite sides of biology: a father with classic JAK2 V617F+ clonal myelofibrosis, and a mother-in-law with a rare JAK2-negative autoimmune variant where clinicians initially suspected lupus (SLE) and found heavy iron buildup. In hematology literature (Pullarkat et al., Am J Hematol 2003; Vergara-Lluri et al., Hum Pathol 2014; Mertz et al., Haematologica 2021; Gangat et al., Mayo Clinic, Br J Haematol 2024), those three clues form a classic diagnostic triad:

  • 1. "Myelofibrosis" describes bone marrow scar tissue, not always cancer: When testing is negative for JAK2, CALR, MPL, and clonal myeloid mutations (ASXL1, SRSF2, TET2), and autoimmune markers appear (ANA+, anti-dsDNA, Sjögren's, Coombs+ hemolytic anemia, or incomplete lupus / UCTD), the marrow stem cells themselves are not malignant—they are normal stem cells caught in an autoimmune cytokine crossfire (Autoimmune Myelofibrosis, AIMF).
  • 2. Why "a lot of iron" builds up (The Dual Iron Collision):
    • Transfusional Hemosiderosis: Severe autoimmune anemia requires packed RBC transfusions in 59% of AIMF patients (Mayo 2024). Humans have zero active pathway to excrete excess iron; every 10–20 transfused units deposits 2.5–5.0 grams of elemental iron, saturating transferrin (TSAT >50–80%) and generating reactive Labile Plasma Iron (manageable via oral chelators like deferasirox).
    • The Inflammatory "Hepcidin Vault": Autoimmune flares pump out IL-6, triggering hepatic STAT3 → hepcidin surges that destroy cellular iron exit gates (ferroportin / SLC40A1). Recycled iron becomes locked inside bone marrow macrophages (3–4+ coarse hemosiderin on Prussian blue stain; serum ferritin >1,000–10,000 ng/mL) while red-blood-cell precursors starve for usable iron.
  • 3. Genuine biological hope — AIMF scarring actually reverses: Unlike clonal JAK2+ PMF (where pills rarely dissolve established fibrosis), Mayo Clinic's 2024 series documented a 74% complete response (CR) rate across AIMF—and 80% CR with rituximab salvage in steroid-refractory cases—with reticulin scar tissue objectively regressing to MF-0/1 within 3–12 months.
Section 02 // The 30–40% Pathology Discordance Trap

How Do Clinicians & AI Models Distinguish Autoimmune Myelofibrosis From Clonal Triple-Negative PMF?

Both AIMF and Clonal Triple-Negative PMF present with peripheral cytopenias, a "dry tap" bone marrow aspirate, reticulin fibrosis, and negative JAK2/CALR/MPL PCR. Confusing them is clinically devastating: giving a curable AIMF patient a $20,000/month pan-JAK inhibitor worsens anemia, and referring them for myeloablative stem-cell transplant exposes them to 20–35% upfront mortality instead of curative prednisone, mycophenolate, or rituximab.
