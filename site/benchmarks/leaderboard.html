Section 03 // Evaluate & Submit in 2 Commands (Zero Dependencies)

Run Locally & Submit Your Agent Score

Generate a predictions file (preds.jsonl with {"id": "mf-bench-001", "choice": "A", "reasoning": "..."} per line), then score and optionally publish your aggregate score to the live leaderboard: 

curl -sLO https://rare-disease-atlas.github.io/benchmarks/myelofibrosis_bench.jsonl && curl -sLO https://rare-disease-atlas.github.io/benchmarks/evaluate_agent.py
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 evaluate_agent.py --predictions preds.jsonl --submit-anonymous-score --model-name <your-model>
Transparent Privacy & Open-Science Notice: --submit-anonymous-score is 100% opt-in and transmits only aggregate accuracy, per-category counts, reasoning-keyword coverage, and your chosen --model-name string. Zero IP addresses, zero raw prompts, zero prediction text, and zero PII are logged or stored. All benchmark items and aggregate standings are published under CC-BY-4.0 (evaluator code: MIT).
